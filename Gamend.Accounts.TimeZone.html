    Gamend.Accounts.TimeZone (gamend_core v1.0.1284)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    A user's time zone, for "their day" and "their evening": an IANA name ("Europe/Bucharest") kept in their private preferences (Gamend.Accounts.Preferences), taken from the browser (Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone, sent on connect) or chosen in settings. Unknown or unset, everything falls back to UTC.

    The tz database is passed to DateTime.shift_zone/3 explicitly, so a host needs no global :time_zone_database config.

    day_start(user, date)

    The moment date begins for a user or a zone, as a UTC DateTime (UTC midnight when the zone is unknown). Where midnight does not exist (a clock change at 00:00), the first moment after it.

    utc (default now) in a user's or a zone's local time; UTC when the zone is unknown.

    local_date(user_or_zone, utc \\ DateTime.utc_now())

    The user's local date (UTC's when their zone is unknown).

    local_hour(user_or_zone, utc \\ DateTime.utc_now())

    The hour (0-23) it is for the user now.

    of(user)

    A user's time zone, or nil when unknown.

    put(user, name)

    Save a user's time zone; an unknown name is refused, the same one is a no-op.

    valid?(name)

    Whether name is a time zone the database knows.
